Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who uses this app

Clip Factory is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.

What Google data it uses

The app asks for one permission: youtube.upload, to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube, Gmail, Drive or Google account data.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token Google returns is saved only on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server run by the app, because there is none. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access on that computer.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
